Retained mockups and saved prompts use anonymous consultancy wording, including older visual revisions. General custom software and SharePoint service context remains available.

### Selection behavior

Scenarios scroll vertically within the list. Client portraits scroll horizontally, with previous and next buttons moving one card at a time. Both collections show part of the next card and a scrollbar to make overflow clear. The selected scenario lead, selected client details, section headings, and Start Simulation button stay in place. Browsing does not change the selected scenario or client; selecting a card does.

The extra scenario names and collection counts illustrate a larger collection. These mockups do not change the agreed MVP content scope.

### Live conversation

The live screen uses three quiet columns: client and conversation on the left, hint and objectives in the middle, and seven skills on the right. It shows Credibility, Confidence, Listening, Rapport, Clarity, Guidance, and Adaptability as slim score bars. The live hint sits above the objectives. All five objectives remain visible in their fixed display order, with the third and fourth completed before the first and second. A current caption sits below the client, with a control for the full transcript. Detailed objective evidence is available on inspection in the proposed interaction.

The shared skill definitions and client-dependent scoring decision are captured in [the proposal](../solutioning/simulator-proposal.md#agreed-default-consulting-and-sales-skills). Scores consider the trainee's behavior and how the conversation is landing; a difficult client can mean lower relevant scores.
