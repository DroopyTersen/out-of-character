# Sam portraits

The male and female versions represent the same warm, perceptive interviewer. Both use the existing cast's large heads, compact waist-up framing, chunky pixels, dark outlines, and simple saturated shading. Matching chestnut hair, teal overshirts, cream shirts and open gestures connect the two versions.

The first pair had realistic proportions and excessive detail. They were replaced using the actual Morgan and Harper sprites as visual references, then the new male Sam as the identity reference for the female version. The PNGs preserve transparent backgrounds.

- Male asset: `public/interview/sam-male.png`
- Female asset: `public/interview/sam-female.png`
- Method: built-in image generation, reference-image edit mode.

## Male prompt

Edit the supplied pixel-art character sprites into a NEW male character named Sam for THE EXACT SAME GAME CAST. Use reference 1 (Morgan) and reference 2 (Harper) as strict art-style and proportion references, not as people to copy. Deliver ONE isolated character portrait, transparent background, square canvas.

CRITICAL: match the references' exaggerated proportions EXACTLY: huge head and expressive big eyes, head from crown to chin approximately HALF the visible figure's total height; short compact shoulders and torso; crop at the belt/waist, no legs. Match the chunky visible pixels, dark stepped outlines, simple 3-5 color shading ramps, bold saturated game-sprite color and crisp blocky pixel clusters of the actual references. This should plausibly be the next asset drawn by the same sprite artist. Do NOT make a realistic-proportioned person, detailed portrait painting, fine textured pixel illustration, tiny head or long torso. Use roughly the same apparent 100-150-pixel-wide character grid as the references even if delivered at larger resolution.

Sam is the approachable male interviewer, around 35-40, warm medium skin, softly swept chestnut-brown wavy hair, large brown eyes with relaxed friendly eyebrows and an easy genuine smile. Light neat stubble, no glasses. A casual teal overshirt with rolled sleeves over a cream T-shirt, no tie or blazer. Relaxed open posture, one hand at waist and the other gently open in a listening gesture. Friendly competent buddy who pays attention, not a stern executive, no exaggerated grin. Three-quarter view facing slightly right but engaging the viewer, symmetrical enough to pair with a female version later. Waist-up composition centered and fills frame, 5% padding top/bottom, same sprite scale as references. No words, props, microphone, furniture, scenic background, drop shadow or watermark. Preserve real transparent alpha around the figure.

## Female prompt

Create the FEMALE VERSION OF SAM using image 1 (the approved male Sam sprite) as the identity, outfit, pose, proportions and palette reference, with image 2 (Harper from the same game) as an additional exact pixel-art style reference. Deliver ONE isolated female character portrait on a truly transparent background, square canvas.

Preserve image 1's art style and proportions EXACTLY: huge head about half the visible total figure height, big kind brown eyes, short compact torso, waist/belt crop with no legs, coarse visible pixel grid, dark stepped pixel outline and bold 3-5 color shading ramps. No realistic head-body proportions, fine texture, smooth illustration, photographic skin, small head or long torso. This must be unmistakably from the same game sprite cast as both reference images.

She is the same approachable thoughtful interviewer Sam, just female: around 35-40, warm medium skin matching male Sam, chestnut brown softly swept wavy hair in an ear-to-chin-length bob, the same friendly brown eye shapes, relaxed expressive eyebrows and warm easy small smile. No beard, no glasses. SAME casual teal overshirt with rolled sleeves and gold buttons over SAME cream T-shirt, same belt, same relaxed open stance: one hand at waist and other gently open in a listening gesture, not a sales pitch. Similar face shape and family resemblance to male Sam, same angle and scale, slightly turned to the right while engaging viewer. Kind attentive buddy, not a glamorous model or stern executive. Centered waist-up with 5% padding, matched height to image 1. No text, props, scenery, furniture, drop shadow, watermark. Preserve actual transparent alpha outside figure.
