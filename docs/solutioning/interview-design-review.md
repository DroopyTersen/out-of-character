Workshop previews use anonymous, synthetic conversation data and make no microphone or paid API calls. Screenshots are in the ignored `output/interview-ui/` directory. The final set is `round3-final/{setup,live,summary}-{1440,390,320}.png`; summary pending and unavailable states are `round3-final/summary-{1440,390,320}-{pending,unavailable}.png`.

The earlier pass (`round2` and `round2-verified`) shortened the setup action and introduction, compacted mobile live controls and readings, added source quotes, and fixed the summary button overflow. A separate review of those screens prompted the final pass below: simpler voice labels, a focused summary column, clearer controls, and accurate pending/unavailable captures. Desktop and mobile were recaptured for each screen after both passes.

These screenshots and behavior checks are static Workshop previews. They do not prove live voice quality, browser permission behavior, or summary generation; those require separate live-session acceptance.
